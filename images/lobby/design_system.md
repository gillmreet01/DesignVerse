# Lobby Transformation Design System & Guidelines

This design system establishes the foundational rules, aesthetics, and technical guidelines for transforming the lobby space across all camera angles and walkthrough positions. Every generated image or physical modification must strictly adhere to these pillars to ensure the virtual experience feels consistent, realistic, premium, and believable.

---

## 1. Core Pillars

### 🏛️ Preservation of Room Architecture
Every generated redesign image must strictly maintain the room's structural anchors. The following elements must remain completely untouched:
- **Original Room Geometry**: The physical room dimensions, wall configurations, and room boundaries are static.
- **Window Placement**: Any window frames and openings must remain in their exact original coordinates.
- **Door Placement**: The central doorways in the back wall must remain in their exact structural positions, keeping their original heights and opening dimensions. Do not wall them off or relocate them.
- **Ceiling Height**: The original vertical clearance, ceiling heights, and outlines are unalterable.
- **Camera Perspective**: Redesign frames and walkthrough nodes must maintain the identical focal length, camera orientation, and perspective angle.
- **Structural Anchors**: The staircase column support post, rails, and right recessed display cabinet alcove cutout are structurally fixed.

*Note: Redesigns must ONLY modify finishes, furniture, lighting, and decor.*

### 📐 Preservation of Room Proportions
- **Visual Scale**: All added furniture, fixtures, and rugs must match the human scale and spatial volume of the room.
- **Passageways**: Maintain clear walking paths (minimum 3 feet visually) between the sofas, the coffee table, and the doorways. Avoid oversized furniture that squeezes the space or makes it look congested.
- **Visual Weight**: Keep furniture profiles balanced. Low-slung contemporary sofas are preferred over bulky high-backed seating to keep sightlines open.

### 🛋️ Realistic Furniture Placement
- **Functional Layout**: Furniture must follow a logical, conversational layout centered around the coffee table.
- **Sink Alignment**: The washbasin/vanity must always align with the plumbing column on the left wall next to the staircase. Do not move the sink to other walls.
- **Armchairs**: Any auxiliary chairs (replacing the plastic chair) must sit naturally in the background corner or next to the main sofas, pointing slightly inward.

### ☀️ Lighting Dynamics
- **Natural Direction**: The primary source of natural ambient light comes from the front-left (where the camera stands at entrance position) and windows. All shadows cast by furniture, doors, and walls must align with this light source.
- **Consistent Tonal Temperature**:
  - **Raw Canvas to Botanical**: Cool, bright natural daylight.
  - **Refined Hearth to Complete**: Warm, soft indoor glows (2700K–3000K) blended with natural ambient light.
- **Synthetic Lights**: Upgraded LED panels, backlit mirrors, and display lights must cast realistic, soft diffuse glows on adjacent surfaces (wood paneling, marble tiles).

### 🛠️ Real-world Achievability
- **Standard Finishes**: All wall paneling, tile choices, and fixtures should reflect commercially available materials (e.g., walnut veneers, Calacatta marble slabs, neutral greige wall paints like Benjamin Moore Revere Pewter).
- **Feasible Trades**: Avoid generating floating objects, impossible cantilevers, or structural modifications that require heavy load-bearing structural engineering changes.

## 2. Six-Tier Design Specifications

### Level 0: Raw Canvas (Original)
- **Visual State**: Touched by daily life. Exposed plumbing, mismatched blankets, everyday visual clutter (garments, bags, papers).
- **Lighting**: Flat, natural ambient lighting.

### Level 1: Decluttered Flow
- **Goal**: Realistic tidying without adding new material costs.
- **Guidelines**: Clear doorways, fold blankets, align sofa pillows, clear surfaces.
- **Flooring**: Clean the floor and smooth out reflections slightly.

### Level 2: Botanical Pathway
- **Goal**: Subtle, organic connection between nature and the interior.
- **Guidelines**: Flush natural stone pathway inlaid into the tile. Indoor ground ferns and leafy plants growing naturally in dedicated soil gaps (no planters/pots).
- **Atmosphere**: Earthy, organic, and highly believable.

### Level 3: Refined Hearth (Minimal)
- **Goal**: Styled functional upgrades.
- **Guidelines**: Replace plastic seating with a simple wood armchair. Replace the basic vanity mirror with a clean black-framed square mirror. Tidy and style the sofa throw pillows.

### Level 4: Modern Harmony (Moderate)
- **Goal**: A warm, contemporary aesthetic transformation.
- **Guidelines**: Soft greige wall paint. Lightweight, matching grey fabric sofa set. Minimalist wooden coffee table. Soft neutral area rug. Floating wood vanity with a backlit LED mirror.

### Level 5: Walnut & Marble Grandeur (Complete)
- **Goal**: High-end modern luxury.
- **Guidelines**: Alternating panels of rich walnut wood veneer and white marble slabs with gold/grey veining. Polished large-format grey marble tiles. Curved velvet designer sofa with brass frames. Round LED backlit vanity mirror. Gold faucets and custom marble vanity top. Warm recessed ceiling spotlights.

## 4. Style Presets

To maintain design continuity across future transformations, implement one of these five curated presets:

### 📐 Modern Minimalist
* **Color Palette**: Monochromatic stark contrast. Crisp gallery white walls, matte black accents, concrete greys, and deep charcoal charcoal.
* **Materials**: Polished concrete flooring, brushed stainless steel, matte black iron, white lacquered surfaces, and clear tempered glass.
* **Furniture Language**: Ultra-low profiles, sharp geometric lines, track arms, legless platform sofas, handleless integrated cabinet hardware, and cantilevered steel tube frames.
* **Lighting Approach**: Cool architectural recessed spotlights (4000K) and concealed indirect linear LED light tracks washing the walls.

### 🌾 Scandinavian
* **Color Palette**: Bright, light, and airy. Muted chalky whites, pale birch wood tone, powder blue, soft sage, and heather grey.
* **Materials**: Light-stained blonde oak, natural pine wood grain, woven wool upholstery, light textured linens, and birch wood veneers.
* **Furniture Language**: Classic Nordic design. Tapered wooden dowel legs, curved bentwood plywood shells, cozy soft-textured upholstery, and high-legged raised furniture pieces.
* **Lighting Approach**: Soft diffuse light (3000K) from iconic paper or painted-metal pendant shades, complemented by cozy corner floor lamps.

### ⛩️ Japandi
* **Color Palette**: Warm, grounding earthy minimalism. Oatmeal, sand, soft charcoal black, warm clay, and cream.
* **Materials**: Bleached white oak, charred Shou Sugi Ban dark wood accents, natural woven paper cord, textured plaster walls, and bamboo matting.
* **Furniture Language**: Blend of Japanese rustic simplicity and Danish warmth. Extremely low-slung, organic curved shapes, woven cord seats, and sliding screens (Shoji-style frames).
* **Lighting Approach**: Soft warm glowing light (2700K). Organic paper lantern pendants (Noguchi-style), hidden up-lights, and low-level soft floor wash lighting.

### 💎 Contemporary Luxury
* **Color Palette**: Deep, dramatic, and glamorous. Calacatta marble white, gold/brass, deep charcoal, emerald green, and emperor purple.
* **Materials**: Polished gold and brushed brass metals, high-gloss lacquered walnut, bookmatched marble panels, plush velvet, and smoked glass.
* **Furniture Language**: Curved voluptuous silhouettes, vertical fluted wood panels, marble-topped coffee tables on brass frames, deep-seated plush velvet seating, and brass plinth bases.
* **Lighting Approach**: Golden light (2700K) casting reflections on marble. Decorative brass wall sconces, integrated under-cabinet LED strip lights, and focused spotlight highlighting.

### 🪴 Warm Organic
* **Color Palette**: Sun-drenched natural earth tones. Terracotta, raw sienna, warm amber, soft sand, and olive green.
* **Materials**: Raw unfilled travertine stone, textured microcement finishes, distressed rustic reclaimed wood, woven rattan, and bouclé fabrics.
* **Furniture Language**: Curved organic outlines, solid wooden block tables, woven wicker/rattan armchairs, and nubby textured fabrics.
* **Lighting Approach**: Warm natural daylight. Fixtures use amber-tinted elements (2200K–2700K) and clay/terracotta pendant lamps casting soft pools of direct downlight.
