Step 2: Room Scan & Structure

We scan the photo for structure, lighting direction, and its dominant colours. Colours are read directly from your image; the notes and estimate below are heuristic guidance, not a measured survey.

Room Analysis Report

Room Type: Residential Lobby / Entrance Lounge
Existing Furniture Inventory: 2x mismatched wooden-frame sofas with throws, 1x low wood coffee table, 1x white plastic armchair, 1x pedestal sink.
Lighting Assessment: Natural cool morning daylight from front-left door openings. Ceiling fan lights cast soft ambient glare. Washbasin corner lacks task lighting.
Color Palette Assessment: Chalky off-white walls, grey/white marbled tile flooring, dark wood trims, purple/pink seating sheet covers (high visual noise).
Improvement Potential (estimate): 58%
Improvement Opportunities:
  • Replace mismatched furniture and covers to unify style.
  • Inlay natural path markers to direct foot traffic flows.
  • Incorporate vertical wood paneling to hide door alignments.
  • Add floating vanity to conceal washbasin pipes.
